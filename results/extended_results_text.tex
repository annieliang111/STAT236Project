With complete contact information, the temporal fitted optimizer has 22.87\% ever infected, compared with 22.76\% for cumulative-history fitting and 25.69\% for the original adaptive heuristic. The paired temporal-minus-cumulative difference is +0.11 percentage points (95\% MC interval [-1.52,+1.73]), which includes zero; therefore the longer simulation still does \emph{not} demonstrate an advantage from recency weighting. Relative to random allocation, the temporal optimizer reduces the mean simulated infection percentage by 12.83 points. With half the dyads visible, its mean infection fraction increases by 4.41 points. These Monte Carlo findings are exploratory because they use only 12 epidemic draws and are conditional on the day-block resampling model.
